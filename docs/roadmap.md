# Roadmap

## Start of the Competitive Season

Launch the first Vibeswap competitive season with zkLTC rewards.

The initial season goal is to introduce a clear monthly competition where players compete on Vibe leaderboards and top wallets qualify for rewards from a predefined zkLTC pool.

Planned season direction:

- One season lasts one month.
- Rewards are distributed among top leaderboard wallets.
- The prize pool is prepared before the season starts.
- Game fees can gradually support future reward pools.
- Season rules and reward distribution should be published before launch.
